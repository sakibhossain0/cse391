01 / Reading

Books on my shelf

I read a bit of everything, but I keep coming back to mysteries. Feluda and Sherlock Holmes are the reason I like detective stories so much, and Dan Brown's puzzle-heavy plots are hard to put down. When I want something quieter, I pick up Dostoevsky's White Nights or Samaresh Majumdar's Garbhadharini.

  1. The Da Vinci CodeDan Brown
  2. Angels & DemonsDan Brown
  3. White NightsFyodor Dostoevsky
  4. The Feluda seriesSatyajit Ray
  5. The Sherlock Holmes storiesArthur Conan Doyle
  6. Garbhadharini (গর্ভধারিণী)Samaresh Majumdar

02 / Writing

Poems and short stories

I write poems and short stories in my spare time. Programming asks me to be exact. Writing lets me be vague on purpose, and I like having both in the same week.

The quote on my home page comes from the same place: a film about a teacher who made his students fall in love with poetry.

05 / Sports

Football, chess and running

Football

The sport I play most. A match with friends is the easiest way to get me away from a screen.

Chess

My slow, quiet sport. Thinking a few moves ahead is good practice for security work too. Free games on Lichess are my go-to.

Marathons

I've run a few marathons. Long runs teach patience in a way that very few other things do.
