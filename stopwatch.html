Stopwatch

A stopwatch that counts in steps of three seconds (3, 6, 9 and so on) and stops on its own at 30 seconds. This is Question 2 of my second CSE391 assignment.

Assignment 2 / Question 2

How it works

  • Counts in threes. The time moves every 3 seconds: 3, 6, 9 and so on, never 1, 2, 3.
  • Stops at 30. At 30 seconds it stops by itself and stays there until you press Reset.
  • Start begins the count. After a Stop, it carries on from the last time shown.
  • Stop pauses the count and keeps the time on the display.
  • Reset stops the count and puts it back to 0.
