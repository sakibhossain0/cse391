CSE370 · Database course project

Pet Adoption Management System

A full-stack web app that brings pets, shelters, adopters, volunteers and admins into one system. Visitors can browse pets, check their medical history, apply to adopt and read success stories. There is also a short quiz that recommends a pet based on your answers.

Most of the work went into the database: an ER model, a normalized relational schema, and the primary and foreign keys that connect eleven modules.

  • Search and browse pets, with profiles and medical records
  • Adoption applications that admins can review
  • Shelter, foster parent and donation management
  • Success stories and an activity log
  • A pet-matching quiz

Team project · Android

BRACU Connect

A companion app for BRAC University students that puts the everyday university stuff in one place. I'm one of the developers on the team, all CSE students at BRACU.

  • CGPA calculator and CGPA analysis
  • Class and exam schedules, plus a deadline tracker
  • Course details and a completed-credits tracker
  • BRACU bus schedule and routes
  • Payment status and a digital ID card
  • Real-time notifications

Latest release: v1.7.4 for Android. An iOS version is in the works.

This website

My homepage for CSE391, and the hub for everything I build in the course this semester. I wrote it by hand in Strict XHTML 1.0, with one shared stylesheet and a small script.

  • Dark and light mode, switched by a CSS-only checkbox toggle
  • A contact form that emails me through FormSubmit
  • Scroll and hover animations that switch off if you prefer reduced motion
  • A responsive class routine table with merged cells
  • Passes the W3C markup and CSS validators
  • XHTML 1.0 Strict
  • CSS3
  • JavaScript
  • Font Awesome
