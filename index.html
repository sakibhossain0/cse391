01 / Who I am

About Me

Brahmanbaria is where I grew up, a town on the banks of the Titas river. I was a science student all the way through school and college. Somewhere in those years the maths and physics turned into curiosity about how computers work, and that is how I ended up in Computer Science and Engineering.

Most of my free time goes into stories. I read (mysteries mostly, plus the odd Russian classic), I write poems and short stories, and I watch more TV series than I would like to admit. Whatever time is left goes into building things: websites, apps, small games, or whatever idea refuses to leave me alone that week.

My long-term goal is to become an AI cybersecurity expert. Attacks keep getting smarter, and I think machine learning will decide who stays ahead of them. I want to be working on that side of the fight.

Things I'm into

  • Reading: Dan Brown, Feluda, Sherlock Holmes, Dostoevsky
  • Writing poems and short stories
  • Films and series, especially slow, character-driven ones
  • Building websites, apps and games
  • Football and chess
  • Running, with a few marathons done
